<h2>Academic background</h2>
<p>I completed my PhD at the University of Leeds through the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Fluid Dynamics and CISTIB. My doctoral research developed efficient ensemble simulation methods for in-silico trials of endovascular medical devices. It included studies of intracranial aneurysm haemodynamics, flow-diverter treatment, and reduced-order approaches for faster vascular-flow simulation.</p>

<p>Alongside research, I contribute through interdisciplinary collaborations, supervision of student projects, and seminar leadership. My technical work brings together mathematical modelling, computational fluid dynamics, fluid-structure interaction, machine learning, and patient-specific simulation.</p>
