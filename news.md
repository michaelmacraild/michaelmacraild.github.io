<h1>Updates</h1>
<p class="lead">Research, presentations, and milestones.</p>

<ul class="news-list news-list-full">
  <li><time datetime="2026-09-16">16 September 2026</time><div><h2>Credibility assessment for computational flow-diverter models</h2><p>I presented an oral talk at the International Clinical Trials Methodology Conference in Birmingham on credibility assessment for computational models of flow-diverter devices in small intracranial aneurysms.</p><p>The presentation introduced in-silico trials and highlighted the FD-PASS and FD-PComA studies, which investigate flow-diverter treatment for intracranial aneurysms. A central focus of the talk was how UK CEiRSI Pilot 2 applied risk-informed credibility frameworks to assess the credibility of the models used in these in-silico trials. This work aims to establish confidence in simulation evidence for medical device development, clinical evaluation, and regulatory decision-making.</p></div></li>
  <li><time datetime="2026-06-05">5 June 2026</time><div><h2>New survey paper on medical image-to-mesh reconstruction</h2><p>Our new <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1361841526002227">survey paper</a> reviews deep-learning approaches for reconstructing three-dimensional anatomical meshes from medical images, covering template, statistical, generative, and implicit models alongside datasets, evaluation methods, current challenges, and future directions. Congratulations to lead author Fengming Lin and the co-authors on its publication.</p></div></li>
