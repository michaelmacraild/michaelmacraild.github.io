  <div><p class="section-label">Reduced-order modelling</p><h2>Neural reduced-order models for aneurysm flow</h2><p>This work developed a non-intrusive parametric model for time-dependent blood flow in intracranial aneurysms. Proper orthogonal decomposition reduces the high-fidelity simulation data, while neural networks predict the reduced coefficients across varying conditions. The best configuration achieved mean test accuracies of 98.6% in the parent vessel and 97.6% in the aneurysm.</p><p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/cnm.3848">Read the paper</a></p></div>
</section>

It compares zero- and one-dimensional models, modal methods, machine-learning-augmented reduced-order models, and physics-informed approaches, identifying where each is effective and where further work is needed for geometrically variable clinical problems.</p><p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2023.0565">Read the paper</a></p></div>
