<p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
<h1>Computational models that can support better clinical decisions.</h1>
<p class="lead">My research focuses on robust simulation workflows for cardiovascular disease and medical-device assessment, from high-fidelity fluid-structure interaction to fast reduced-order approximations.</p>

<h2>In-silico trials</h2>
<p>I investigate how virtual patient cohorts can complement conventional evidence generation, particularly for endovascular treatment of intracranial aneurysms. This includes modelling flow-diverter performance in patient-specific vascular geometries and contributing to credible regulatory pathways for computational models.</p>

<h2>Cardiovascular modelling</h2>
<p>I work with computational fluid dynamics and fluid-structure interaction to study haemodynamics in intracranial aneurysms and severe aortic stenosis. Current interests include automated, patient-specific pipelines that make advanced simulations practical at scale.</p>

<h2>Accelerated simulation</h2>
<p>I develop and assess reduced-order modelling and machine-learning methods for vascular flow modelling. My work has covered proper orthogonal decomposition, neural networks, and physics-informed learning, with an emphasis on preserving useful accuracy while reducing computational cost.</p>

<p class="note">Expertise includes mathematical modelling, computational fluid dynamics, fluid-structure interaction, reduced-order modelling, physics-informed machine learning, haemodynamics, intracranial aneurysms, aortic stenosis, and in-silico trials.</p>
